## 🌟 Featured projects

### 🌙 HAVEN — Celestial interactive world
An original Godot-based world with Sun, Moon, and Star companions, explorable environments, and customizable personal spaces. Designed for touch/click interaction.

**Status:** Prototype and integration in progress. Android release and in-app purchases are planned, not launched. Model-backed companion AI remains a development goal; scripted interactions are not generative AI.

### 🧠 AHI — Human–AI representation research
Investigating whether AI can translate an individual's prior reasoning into formal representations without introducing new reasoning steps, and how to test that constraint.

**Status:** Research hypothesis and prototype work; public reproducibility and independent validation pending.

### 🛠️ ORION — Supervised agent engineering
Exploring coordinated AI-assisted engineering with bounded permissions, test gates, and human review.

**Status:** Private development; public demonstration planned.

### 🔎 I SPY — Evidence-first research
Separating primary sources, established facts, competing explanations, and open questions.

**Status:** Research method in development.
